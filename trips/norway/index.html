Packing Essentials

Weather & Clothing

August in Norway and London can be cool and rainy. Pack waterproof jackets, layers, comfortable hiking boots, hat, gloves, and an umbrella.

Essentials

Don't forget: Passport & travel documents, travel insurance, power adapters (UK/EU), medications & first aid, and camera.

Practical Information

Travel Documents & Visas

US citizens can visit Norway and the UK visa-free for up to 6 months. Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates.

Currency & Money

Norway uses Norwegian Krone (NOK) and the UK uses British Pounds (GBP). Both countries widely accept credit/debit cards.

Language

English is widely spoken in both countries. Communication is easy for travelers.

Best Time to Visit

August is ideal with long daylight hours and mild temperatures in Norway. London offers various festivals and outdoor events.
