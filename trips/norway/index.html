Packing Essentials

Weather & Clothing

August in Norway and London can be cool and rainy. Pack:

  • Waterproof jacket and pants
  • Layers (sweaters, long-sleeved shirts)
  • Comfortable hiking boots
  • Hat and gloves
  • Umbrella

Essentials

Don't forget:

  • Passport & travel documents
  • Travel insurance
  • Power adapters (UK/EU)
  • Medications & first aid
  • Camera & memory cards

Practical Information

Travel Documents & Visas

US citizens can visit Norway and the UK visa-free for up to 6 months. Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates.

Currency & Money

Norway uses Norwegian Krone (NOK) and the UK uses British Pounds (GBP). Notify your bank of travel dates. Both countries widely accept credit/debit cards.

Language

English is widely spoken in both countries. In Norway and London, most people speak English fluently, making communication easy for travelers.

Best Time to Visit

August is an ideal time to visit Norway with long daylight hours and mild temperatures. London is pleasant in August with various festivals and outdoor events happening throughout the city.
