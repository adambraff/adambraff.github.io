Bergen Highlights (Days 5–7)

Day 6 Thu Aug 27 — Bergen: Adventure Day ▼
TimeActivityNotes
Plan A: Mountain + water
MorningUlriken cable car + hike down to FløyenCable car to Ulriken (643m) — highest of Bergen's seven mountains. Panoramic views. Then hike the Vidden trail toward Mt. Fløyen (~3 hrs, moderate, well-marked). Or just ride up and back if the group prefers.
MiddayFløibanen funicular down from Mt. Fløyen7-min ride back to city center from 320m. Runs every 15 min. Completes the mountain traverse.
LunchFish Market (Fisketorget)Fresh shrimp, king crab legs, fish soup. Eat at the outdoor stalls — casual, fun, right on the harbor.
AfternoonCoastal kayaking2–3 hr guided paddle in Bergen's harbor and islands. Different vibe from fjord kayaking but scenic and active. Book via Bergen Sea Kayak or similar.
DinnerCornelius or LolaCornelius = take a boat to a seafood restaurant on an island — adventure + dinner in one. Lorrie & Emma pick. Lola = another family pick. Or Lysverket (1 Michelin star, KODE museum) or Gaptrast (1 Michelin star, New Nordic, charcoal fire).
Plan B: Skip the big hike. Fløibanen funicular + easy trails at the top of Fløyen in the morning. Fish Market for lunch. Afternoon: Bergen Sea Kayak or a boat tour of the harbor islands. Same dinner plan — Lysverket or Gaptrast.
🌧️ Bergen rain plan: Bergen averages rain ~60% of August days. If it's pouring: KODE Art Museums (4 buildings, includes Lysverket for lunch), Bergen Aquarium, or Bryggens Museum (medieval archaeology under Bryggen). Fløibanen still runs in rain but skip it if visibility is zero — the view IS the point. Move adventure day to Friday and swap with the cooking experience.
Active experiences are this family's top preference. The Ulriken→Fløyen traverse is Bergen's signature hike with incredible views the whole way. Two anchors (hike + kayak) is the sweet spot — three is too many.
⚽ Sports watch: ~35% chance SK Brann has a home match this week at Brann Stadion (~17,000). Three Bergen nights improves your odds vs. the old 2-night plan. Brann fans are famously passionate.

Cooking experiences scored top-5 on every past trip, so Version A is the default. The fishing experience is a half-day commitment — don't stack a heavy dinner on top. Having 3 Bergen nights means you can flex the schedule around weather without losing anything.
