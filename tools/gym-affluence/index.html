Approach: Where Gyms Choose to Locate

Premium gyms are selective about locations — Equinox operates in just 14 states. Budget chains blanket the country. The bubble chart below plots each chain by the weighted-average income of states where it operates (x-axis), its average monthly dues (y-axis), and number of locations (bubble size). If the hypothesis holds, premium chains should cluster in the upper-right quadrant.

Approach: How Much Do Different Income Groups Spend on Fitness?

The Bureau of Labor Statistics Consumer Expenditure Survey (CEX) tracks what Americans spend by income quintile. The "Fees and admissions" category (gym memberships, sporting events, movies, and other paid recreation) shows a massive income gradient — extracted directly from the 2023 BLS Table 1101 Excel file.

Fees & Admissions as % of Income by Quintile

Even as a share of income, the top quintile spends slightly more — but the ratio compresses dramatically compared to absolute dollars. This means wealthier households are buying more expensive recreational experiences, not just spending proportionally.

Nuance: Q1 spends 1.1% of income on fees & admissions vs. Q5 at 1.0%. In absolute dollars it's a 16x gap, but as an income share it's nearly flat. The real story is that Q5 households can afford $229/month on recreation fees — enough for premium gym memberships, concert tickets, and club dues — while Q1 households have $14/month total for everything in this category.
