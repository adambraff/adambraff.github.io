Methodology & Assumptions

This dashboard applies Tetlockian superforecasting principles to estimate the probability that HJR 203 (or a substantively similar joint resolution) both passes the Florida Legislature and is approved by 60%+ of voters in November 2026.

  • Base rate: FL constitutional amendments proposed by the legislature requiring 60% voter approval have a mixed track record. Amendment 4 (abortion, 2024) got 57% and failed.
  • Decomposition: P(Senate passes) × P(on ballot | passes) × P(voters approve at 60%). Each conditional estimated independently.
  • Financial signals: JOE, XHB, MUB, CPT, EQR, NAD data pulled live from Alpha Vantage (free tier, 5 req/min). All charts show ratios oriented so higher = more likely passage.
  • Manifold Market: Live probability from Manifold Markets API (CORS-friendly, no key). Low liquidity caveat applies.
  • Muni proxy: NAD (Nuveen Quality Municipal Income Fund, NYSE CEF) used as muni market proxy. All FL-specific muni funds (FRFLX, VFLTX, MAFMX, MFFLX) were liquidated in 2025. MUB/NAD ratio captures muni CEF discount stress.
  • Polling: Three polls with varying methodology. UNF PORL (49%) most neutral. JMI (65%) favorable framing. 60% threshold is genuinely hard.
  • Key uncertainties: (1) Whether DeSantis calls a special session; (2) Senate willingness to negotiate; (3) Opposition campaign effectiveness.

Data freshness: Financial data and Manifold probability refresh on page load and via the "Refresh Market Data" button. Polls, timeline events, and forecast log are manually maintained in the source code.
