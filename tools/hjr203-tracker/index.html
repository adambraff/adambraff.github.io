Methodology & Assumptions

This dashboard applies Tetlockian superforecasting principles to estimate the probability that HJR 203 (or a substantively similar joint resolution) both passes the Florida Legislature and is approved by 60%+ of voters in November 2026.

  • Base rate: Florida constitutional amendments proposed by the legislature that require 60% voter approval have a mixed track record. The 60% threshold is genuinely hard — Amendment 4 (abortion, 2024) got 57% and failed.
  • Decomposition: We break the chain into P(Senate passes) × P(on ballot | passes) × P(voters approve at 60%). Each conditional is estimated independently with Bayesian updating.
  • Financial signals: FL muni bond spreads, JOE stock performance, and REIT spreads are noisy proxies. They capture market sentiment but are confounded by interest rates, migration, and macro conditions. Use as directional confirmation, not primary evidence.
  • Polling: Three polls exist with varying methodology. The UNF PORL poll (49% support) is the most methodologically neutral. JMI (65%) uses favorable framing. The 60% threshold means even optimistic polls barely clear.
  • Key uncertainties: (1) Whether DeSantis calls a special session; (2) Senate willingness to negotiate; (3) Opposition campaign effectiveness on service cuts messaging.
