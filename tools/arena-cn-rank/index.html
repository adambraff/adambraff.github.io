How to read this

Rank got worse while rating got better. The top Chinese model gained ~27 Elo points over six months and finished ranked lower than it started. The field grew from 293 to 374 models, and most of the new arrivals above it were US variants — thinking modes, sizes, checkpoints. Rank here is partly measuring US release cadence.

Preview models distort the top. Several leaders took the Chinese #1 slot on fewer than 5,000 votes with ±10 confidence intervals, then decayed as samples grew. preliminary marks runs that peaked under 5k votes. qwen3.7-max-preview has sat at ~3,700 votes since May 19 — its vote count is falling, so that estimate will not sharpen.

The gap is inside the noise for recent months. Frontier-to-Chinese closed from ~57 points in January to low-30s by April. Everything after that is within overlapping confidence intervals. Treat the recent flat stretch as “no measurable change,” not as a plateau you can date.

No rebaseline artifact. The lmarena.ai → arena.ai transition on Jan 22–31 shifted 298 common models by a median of +0.01 points. The series is continuous across it.
