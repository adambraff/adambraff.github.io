# Giants at Commanders Playbook

An interactive, educational game-day guide for the New York Giants at Washington Commanders, Week 5 of the 2026 NFL season (Sunday, October 11, 2026, Northwest Stadium).

Live: https://adambraff.github.io/tools/nyg-was-playbook/

## What's in it

- **Plays**: 13 animated situations drawn on a whiteboard field (Giants offense, Commanders offense, special teams). Tap any player for his role on that play and who he is; each play has a short explanation and rough outcome odds.
- **Personnel**: decoder for 10, 11, 12, 13, 21 and 22 personnel, with the typical defensive response and how each team uses it.
- **Glossary**: 30 football terms (formations, run and pass concepts, pressure, coverage shells) with mini diagrams.
- **Coaches**: tendency cards for both coordinators and head coaches, with a tell to watch for each.
- **Props**: game lines and player props converted to implied probabilities, with a plain-English read on each.
- **Game script**: pregame win probability with toggles for the main swing factors.
- **Who's who** and **What to watch**: projected starters with injury status, and what to look for from the stands.

## Notes

- Single self-contained `index.html`; vanilla JavaScript and inline SVG, no build step. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
- Data reflects the Friday (Oct 9) injury report and betting lines as of Oct 9-10, 2026. Play diagrams are illustrative reconstructions of each staff's tendencies; play-level odds and win-probability adjustments are estimates.
