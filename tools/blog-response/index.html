Blog Response

One click on the GoDaddy Customers page: who opened, clicked, bounced or unsubscribed from the latest braff.co post.

Setup, once: drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

Blog response
After each post (wait a day or two for opens to settle):
  1. Click Blog response in your bookmarks bar. If you are not already on GoDaddy's Customers page, it takes you there; sign in if asked, then click the bookmark again.
  2. Keep the tab in front for about 30 seconds while it pages through your contacts.
  3. Read the report in the panel. Download CSV saves every contact's latest activity.

Runs entirely in your browser, using your own GoDaddy login. It reads the contact list the same way you would by paging through it; nothing is sent anywhere. Each contact counts once, at their latest action, so run it before your next post goes out. Opens are inflated by Apple Mail privacy preloading.
