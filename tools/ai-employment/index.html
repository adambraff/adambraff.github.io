Data Sources & Methodology

Historical data: BLS Current Population Survey via FRED (LFPR, UNRATE, EMRATIO series). Annual averages 2000–2025.
AI forecasts: WEF Future of Jobs 2025 (92M displaced / 170M created by 2030), McKinsey (30% work hours automatable by 2030), Goldman Sachs (300M jobs globally at risk), IMF (60% exposure in advanced economies).
Industry exposure: McKinsey industry-level automation potential, WEF sectoral outlook, MIT 2025 study (11.7% directly automatable today).
Model: S-curve AI adoption × industry exposure → task displacement → lagged unemployment effect, with job creation offset and demographic drift. Probability bands generated via parametric uncertainty propagation (not Monte Carlo). Central projection is median; bands are symmetric around it scaled by the uncertainty parameter.
Limitations: This is a scenario tool, not a forecast. It aggregates published estimates and lets you explore "what if" — treat wide bands as a feature, not a bug. Base rates and outside-view thinking apply: historically, automation waves produce transitory unemployment spikes followed by recovery with higher productivity.
