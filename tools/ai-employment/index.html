Data Sources & Methodology

Historical data: BLS Current Population Survey via FRED (LFPR, UNRATE, EMRATIO series). Annual averages 2000–2025.
Prediction markets: Kalshi unemployment spike contracts ($189K volume), Polymarket unemployment distribution ($64K volume), Metaculus AI-automatable jobs (resolves May 2026).
Financial indicators: CME FedWatch (Fed Funds futures), BLS JOLTS (job openings), Conference Board Consumer Confidence, University of Michigan Sentiment, CBOE VIX.
AI forecasts: WEF Future of Jobs 2025, McKinsey, Goldman Sachs, IMF.
Model: Near-term (years 1-3) blends market-implied unemployment path with scenario model using configurable weight. Market-implied values derived from Kalshi/Polymarket CDF inversion: P(>5%)=37%, P(>6%)=17% → implied median ~4.8%. Long-term (years 4-10) uses S-curve AI adoption × industry exposure → task displacement → lagged unemployment, with job creation offset and demographic drift. Probability bands: near-term widths from market-implied volatility (Kalshi spread), long-term from parametric uncertainty propagation.
Limitations: This is a scenario tool, not a forecast. Prediction market liquidity is thin ($64-189K) — treat as informed priors, not gospel. Ensemble thinking applies: the market anchor keeps you honest on the near-term while your scenario assumptions drive the longer horizon where markets have no contracts.
