Day 4 Tue Aug 25 — Bergen Railway + Flåm Railway 🚂 ▼

Flåm & Fjord Area Detail

Flåm Railway Fjord cruise Boat to Undredal Drive to Stegastein
TimeActivityNotes
7:30 AMBergen Railway: Oslo S → Myrdal~5 hrs. One of the world's great train rides. Crosses Hardangervidda plateau. Book window seats.
~12:30Arrive Myrdal (867m)Transfer to Flåm Railway. 15–20 min connection.
~1:00 PMFlåm Railway: Myrdal → Flåm~1 hr. 20 km descent, 863m drop. Waterfalls, tunnels, hairpin bends. Steepest standard-gauge rail in the world.
~2:00 PMArrive Flåm, check inFlåmsbrygga Hotel (fjord-view balconies, 8.6/10) or Fretheim Hotel (historic).
AfternoonStegastein Viewpoint30m cantilevered platform, 650m above Aurlandsfjord. 10 min drive. Jaw-dropping.
DinnerÆgir BryggeriViking-themed brewpub in Flåm. Craft beer, hearty food. Fun, not stuffy.
The transit IS the experience. Bergen Railway + Flåm Railway are top-5 train rides on Earth. No "wasted" travel day.

Day 7 Fri Aug 28 — Bergen: Cooking Class + Highlights 🐟 ▼
TimeActivityNotes
MorningFishing + outdoor cooking experienceHalf-day with local guide: catch your own fish, cook it outdoors overlooking the fjord. ~4 hrs total.

⚽ Sports watch: ~25% chance SK Brann has a home match Thu–Fri at Brann Stadion (~17,000). Lower odds with only 2 nights. Brann fans are famously passionate.
