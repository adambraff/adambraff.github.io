TimeActivityNotes

Logistics & Planning

Accommodation

  • Oslo (Aug 4–7): 3 nights. Central location (Sentrum or Bjørvika). ~1100–1500 NOK/night
  • Flåm (Aug 7–9): 2 nights. Riverside hotel. ~1200–1600 NOK/night
  • Bergen (Aug 9–11): 2 nights. Bryggen or harbor area. ~1200–1600 NOK/night
  • London (Aug 11–15): 4 nights. West End or South Bank. £100–150/night

Transportation

  • Flights: SFO → Oslo (Icelandair or direct); Bergen → London (SAS, Lufthansa); London → SFO (direct or hub)
  • Trains: Oslo Central → Flåm Railway (via Myrdal). Flåm → Bergen ferry or train via Voss
  • Local: Buses, trams in cities. London Oyster card recommended
  • Car rental: Optional for flexibility in Norway; not needed in London (too congested)

Booking Priority

  • Flights (Icelandair, SAS, Norwegian Air, Lufthansa)
  • Hotels (3–6 months ahead for summer)
  • Flåm Railway (seasonal, books out)
  • Ferry Flåm→Bergen (if available; confirm dates)
  • Attractions (Westminster Abbey, Tower pre-book if time-slot important)

Budget (Estimate)

  • Flights: $700–1000 per person (transatlantic + regional)
  • Hotels: ~$4500 total (14 nights avg)
  • Food: ~$1500 (mix casual & nice dinners)
  • Activities: ~$500 (museums, ferries, cable car)
  • Misc: ~$300 (tips, transit, emergencies)
  • Total/person: ~$2300–2800

Packing Tips

  • Norway: Waterproof jacket, layers, comfortable walking shoes
  • August weather: 12–18°C in Norway; 15–20°C in London. Rain likely
  • Gear: Sunscreen, adapters (UK plugs), small backpack for day trips
  • Docs: Passport, travel insurance, hotel confirmations on phone

Practical Notes

  • Currency: NOK (Norway), GBP (UK). Cards widely accepted everywhere
  • Language: English widely spoken in both countries
  • Phones: EU roaming works; consider local SIM for Norway/UK if heavy usage
  • Visas: US citizens visa-free (90 days Schengen/UK)
